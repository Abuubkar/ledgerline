## Recommendation (2026-11-01, prompts `extract-page/4` and `agent/1`)

**Keep `gemma4:12b` as the default.** It passes every gate with no failing header field, 99.3% of lines, and every
agent action right. The cost is speed (73 invoices/h) and memory (8.7 GB of 16 GB).

- **qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M is the strongest alternative.** Reads as well as gemma (99.0% header, 99.3% lines), 14 of 15
  agent actions, 1.6× gemma's speed, 2.1 GB less memory. Its gaps:
  - **Agent:** one credit memo where the goods weren't received (should hold); hold reasons right only 62.5% of the
    time.
  - **Tax ID:** misses the Japanese registration number on one layout.
  - It would be the choice if speed or memory starts to matter more than one agent scenario in 15.
- **qwen2.5:7b-instruct is the fastest (172 invoices/h, 5.1 GB) and reads lines as well as the others**, but proposes
  the wrong action in 5 of 15 agent scenarios (holds where a credit memo is due) and misses "Net" as a subtotal on
  one layout. Extraction only, if ever.
