## Recommendation (2026-11-01, prompts `extract-page/3` and `agent/1`)

**Keep `gemma4:12b` as the default.** It's the only model that passes every gate, and the only one with every agent
action right. It's also the slowest (74 invoices/h) and the biggest (8.7 GB on a 16 GB machine).

- **qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M is the strongest alternative.** Best line items (99.3%), 14 of 15 agent actions, 1.5× gemma's
  speed, 1.6 GB less memory. Its gaps:
  - **Subtotal (72.5%).** On pages that print no subtotal it reports its own sum of that page's lines, and the
    pages then disagree. That breaks the prompt's "never compute" rule; a prompt change aimed at it is next
    (`extract-page/4`).
  - **Agent:** one credit memo where goods weren't received (should hold), and hold reasons often `other`.
  - Re-decide after `extract-page/4`.
- **qwen2.5:7b-instruct reads invoices nearly as well as gemma at 2.1× the speed**, but proposes the wrong action in 5
  of 15 agent scenarios: after gathering the right evidence it holds instead of drafting a credit memo. Fine for
  extraction only. Splitting the jobs between two models would mean loading and unloading on every switch, which
  isn't worth it on 16 GB.
- **gemma4 changed too.** Given the choice, it writes amounts as bare numbers (558 of 558). Totals are now 100%, but
  on 4 invoices it took the "5" in "A4 copy paper, 5 reams" as the quantity, so line F1 fell from 97.8% to 96.4%.
  `extract-page/4` addresses that as well.
